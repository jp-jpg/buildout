The curve moved up as a block, and real yields did the lifting.

Mechanism

Two kinds of AI selloff

Whether long Treasuries help depends on why the book is falling. Long yields react to the cause of the selloff, and the two causes push them in opposite directions.

Growth scare

Bonds hedge
  1. AI revenue or demand disappoints
  2. Growth and rate expectations fall
  3. Long yields drop
  4. TLT rallies while the book falls

Oct 8, 2026. Reports of weak OpenAI revenue hit AI names; the Nasdaq-100 fell 1.39%. The 30-year yield fell 5.9bp, worth about +0.9% on a bond with TLT's 15.5-year duration.

investingLive

Financing or inflation shock

Bonds fail
  1. Oil, capex debt or fiscal supply push inflation and issuance up
  2. Term premium rises; the Fed hikes
  3. Long yields climb
  4. TLT falls with the book

March 2026. As the Iran war closed the Strait of Hormuz, the S&P 500 fell 5.1% and a 10-year Treasury position lost about 2.0% in the same month. Dec 12, 2025. The Nasdaq-100 fell 1.9% on AI capex worries while the 10-year yield rose 4bp.

Federal Reserve · Plastics Industry Association · Bloomberg via Swissinfo

A hedge removes at most ρ² of the book's variance: 9% at a −0.3 correlation. Long bonds protect against a scenario; options and lower gross protect against the drawdown itself.

Rules

Survival rules the engine enforces

  1. Ruin is the only unrecoverable loss.Size so the 95th-percentile one-year drawdown stays inside the limit, before any view on return. (Taleb's ergodicity point: a bet you cannot repeat forever is not worth its average.)
  2. Barbell the book.Very safe on one side (bills, TIPS), very convex on the other (options, the concentrated AI sleeve). Avoid the fragile middle: leveraged carry that loses everything in one gap.
  3. Prefer contracts to correlations.A put pays by contract. A bond hedge pays only if the correlation holds, and it flipped in 2022 and again in 2026.
  4. Trim on rules.A position that runs 20% over its target weight gets cut back to target. Sell in pieces; never all or nothing.
  5. Know the rate exposure every day.Every name carries a rates beta; the book's total is reported daily next to the hedge ratio.

Insurers & balance sheets

The market's long-duration buyers are becoming the AI buildout's lenders.

Insurers collect premiums today and pay claims years later. That float makes them natural buyers of long-dated debt and, increasingly, of private credit and data-center bonds. Watch them for two signals: where long-end demand comes from, and where credit risk is quietly piling up.

Life and annuity liabilities run decades, so insurers buy long bonds and private loans to match them. That makes them price-setters for the long end.

Desk principles

From a market maker's advice

Notes from a conversation with a trader who built his own prop firm and market-making business.

  1. Process over P&L.Nobody cares that you were up 15% last year. They want to see the system they could plug into theirs.
  2. Data first, then judgment.Build the tool, use it, then ask whether the answer makes sense.
  3. Trim, don't dump.Break positions into pieces; a winner can be cut 20% without an all-or-nothing call.
  4. Always know your rate exposure.Watch long bonds against growth stocks; the relationship decides whether your hedge exists.
  5. Speed of learning is the edge.Pattern recognition and a fast observe-decide-act loop beat any single model.
  6. Choose who you go to battle with.Proximity to sharp people compounds faster than any position.

System

The engine behind the desk.

Python, about 3,500 lines including tests, run every weekday on GitHub Actions. One timing rule runs through all of it: a value at date t uses data through the close of t, and a decision made at t earns returns from t+1.

  1. Data

    FRED curve, credit, volatility and oil daily; Databento bars with split and dividend factors; CME Treasury futures; SEC filings; Kalshi odds.

  2. Exposures

    Daily betas to market, AI and rates factors. SPCX leans on TSLA's betas until it has its own history.

  3. Regime

    Stock-bond correlation, exponentially weighted with a 63-day half-life, read with hysteresis: hedging, neutral or amplifying.

  4. Hedge & size

    Min-variance TLT ratio when the regime allows it. Vol target and drawdown cap set gross; Kelly checks the edge.

  5. Prove

    Dollar-exact backtest with costs and financing, deflated Sharpe, and walk-forward forecasts judged on calibration.
